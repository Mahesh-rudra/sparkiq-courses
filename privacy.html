Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information Vedax Global ("we") collects when you use learn.vedaxglobal.com (the SparkIQ courses) and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Email address that you enter before paying. We use it to find your payment if you need help.
  • Payment details held by Razorpay such as your mobile number, payment ID, payment method and amount. Razorpay processes your payment under its own privacy policy. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
  • Messages you send us when you email help@vedaxglobal.com.
  • Access code saved on your device. After you enter your code, your browser stores it (in local storage) so the lessons stay unlocked on that device. It is not sent to any advertiser.

We do not ask for your name, address, date of birth or any other personal details to use the free or paid lessons.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm your payment and give you access to the Course.
  • To answer your questions and give support, for example if you lose your code.
  • To keep records needed for tax, accounting and legal reasons, including GST invoices.
  • To prevent misuse, such as sharing of access codes.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who processes it for us

  • Razorpay processes payments.
  • Cloudflare runs the small server that creates your order and checks your payment.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the text fonts, so your browser may contact Google servers when a page loads.

These providers handle data under their own policies.

4. Cookies and tracking

We do not run advertising cookies or analytics. The only item stored on your device is the access code described above. Razorpay may set its own cookies during checkout.

5. Keeping information

We keep payment and invoice records for as long as tax and accounting rules require. Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you.

6. Your choices

  • You can clear the stored access code at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can then enter the code again.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, except where we must keep it by law. Email help@vedaxglobal.com.

7. Children

The Courses suit students of all ages. If you are under 18, please buy with a parent's or guardian's help.

8. Security

Payments go through Razorpay's secure checkout. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no online service can be completely secure.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. For questions or complaints, write to help@vedaxglobal.com. We aim to reply within 2 working days.
