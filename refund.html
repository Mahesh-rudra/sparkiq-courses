Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers cancellations and refunds for the SparkIQ Arduino courses sold by Vedax Global.

In short: the Courses are digital and you get access as soon as you pay, so we do not give refunds once access is given. The free lessons let you try the Courses first. We do refund in the cases listed below.

1. Cancellation

You can stop before you pay, at any point in checkout. Once payment is confirmed and your access code is shown, the order cannot be cancelled.

2. When we do refund

  • Double payment: you were charged more than once for the same Course. We refund the extra payment.
  • Payment taken but no access: money left your account but you did not receive an access code and we cannot give you one.
  • Technical problem: the Course does not work for you because of a fault on our side, and we cannot fix it within 7 days of your report.

3. When we do not refund

  • You changed your mind, or bought the wrong level.
  • You did not finish the Course, or the content was not what you expected. Please try the free lessons before buying.
  • Your device or browser is not supported.
  • Your access code was shared, copied or misused.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email help@vedaxglobal.com within 7 days of your payment. Include your payment ID (shown on the screen after payment and in the Razorpay receipt), the email you used, and a short description of the problem. We will reply within 2 working days.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay. They usually reach your account within 5 to 7 working days, depending on your bank. Payment gateway fees are not returned to us for refunded payments, but you receive the full amount you paid.
